Privacy Policy for RepGym AI

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to RepGym AI ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have in relation to your personal data when you use our mobile application (RepGym AI).

1. Information We Collect

When you use RepGym AI, we collect the following types of information to provide personalized fitness training and workout planning:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, profile photo, and authentication credentials (via Google Sign-In or email authentication).
  • Fitness & Health Data: Height, body weight, fitness goals (e.g., muscle gain, fat loss), workout experience level, available equipment, target muscle groups, and training frequency.
  • Workout Logs & Progress: Logged exercises, sets, reps, weights lifted, personal records (PRs), workout duration, and history.
  • Step Counter & Activity Data: Daily step counts collected from your device's built-in step sensors with your permission to track daily caloric expenditure.
  • AI Chat Messages: Questions, prompt queries, and workout guidance conversations with the AI Coach.
  • Device & Usage Information: Device model, OS version, language preference, app crash diagnostics, and anonymous analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to provide, maintain, and optimize your fitness experience:

  • To generate tailored multi-week workout splits and daily gym routines.
  • To provide instant real-time advice on exercise form, recovery, and meal guidance via the AI Gym Coach.
  • To track and visualize your strength progression, volume history, and daily fitness streaks.
  • To process subscription entitlements and restore purchases via RevenueCat and Google Play Billing.
  • To send workout reminders, hydration alerts, and rest notifications (if enabled).

3. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Processing

Gemini AI Integration: RepGym AI utilizes Google Gemini API models to analyze your fitness preferences and generate workout routines and coach responses. Your prompts and fitness profile inputs are transmitted securely to Google Gemini for real-time inference only. We do NOT sell your personal workout data, and your inputs are not used to train public AI models without your consent.

4. Third-Party Services We Use

We partner with industry-standard, secure third-party service providers to power key app features:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure user authentication, cloud database sync, and crash reporting. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Gemini API: Used for AI workout plan generation and conversational coaching. Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat & Google Play Billing: Used to securely manage in-app subscriptions, receipts, and entitlement verification. RevenueCat Privacy Policy

5. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

RepGym AI offers optional auto-renewing subscriptions (RepGym Pro). All financial transactions and payment card details are handled directly and securely by Google Play Store Billing. We never receive or store your credit card or payment credentials.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your personal fitness data only for as long as your account remains active. You have full control over your data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated fitness logs at any time in the app via Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
  • Manual Deletion Request: You can request complete data erasure by emailing us at support@tekzovix.com. Upon receipt, all stored records in our databases will be permanently purged within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

RepGym AI is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that a child has provided us with personal information, we will immediately delete it from our servers.

8. Security of Your Data

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures, including HTTPS encryption in transit and AES encryption at rest, to safeguard your personal information.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document and through an in-app notice when appropriate.
